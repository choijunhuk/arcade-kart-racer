# Generated placeholder audio — CC0

All WAV files in this directory are original, deterministic procedural sounds
generated by `tools/gen_placeholder_audio.gd` for Turbo Circuit. No recordings,
samples, melodies, or downloads from third parties were used.

audio files to the public domain under CC0 1.0 Universal:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

They may be copied, modified, distributed, and used commercially without
attribution. They are provided without warranty. Final assets replace these
placeholders in Phase 13.
