# Original procedural art — CC0-1.0

The project dedicates the original generated PNGs in this directory, the schematic
previews in ../previews, and the procedural kart/track/particle artwork generated
by the repository scripts to the public domain under CC0 1.0 Universal.
No downloaded models, textures, system fonts, or third-party artwork were used.
Generator sources: tools/generate_ui_art.gd, tools/generate_previews.gd,
kart/kart_mesh_builder.gd, tools/track_art.gd, effects/particle_art.gd.
Godot and the default font retain their upstream licenses.
